# Papers and research synthesis · 论文与研究总结

Papers summarize multiple experiments; they are not themselves experimental
evidence. The canonical status of each underlying study is recorded in
[`experiments/`](../experiments/).

论文用于综合多个实验，不应被当作新的实验结果。每项底层实验的状态以
[`experiments/`](../experiments/) 为准。

## Current bilingual paper · 当前双语论文

**Question:** Does removing one measurable anomaly change an LLM's proposed
next experiment, and is that proposal repeatable under identical evidence?

**Answer:** The broad direction changed from temporal history to spatial
geometry, but the two full-evidence repetitions selected different
interventions. The result is evidence-sensitive but not stable and does not
establish scientific novelty.
